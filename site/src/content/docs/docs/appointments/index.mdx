Appointments are interviews with the bishop or a counselor: temple recommends, youth interviews, extending a calling and more. Ward Desk keeps the whole path in one place, so nothing falls through the gaps between the bishopric and the secretary.

## What you can do

- Ask for an interview, from Home or in the bishopric meeting.
- Find a time that fits the leader's real hours, and offer it by text or email.
- Send reminders the day before, if the secretary chooses to.
- Have the leader mark each one done, or missed and sent back.

## How it works

1. **Someone needs to be seen.** The bishop or a counselor asks for an interview from Home, or approves a name for a calling. The person appears under **Needs an appointment**, with no time yet.
2. **A secretary finds a time.** Open times come from the leader's hours, minus away dates and anything already booked. The secretary offers a few times or books one. See [Book an appointment](/docs/appointments/book-an-appointment/).
3. **It moves to the leader's Home.** Once booked, the appointment is the leader's.
4. **A reminder goes out the day before**, unless the secretary has turned reminders off. See [Reminders](/docs/appointments/reminders/).
5. **The leader reports.** After the interview, they mark it done, or missed. See [After the interview](/docs/appointments/after-the-interview/).

## Where appointments are

- **Appointments tab** (executive secretaries): everything **To schedule** and **Scheduled**, for everyone or just one leader, a way to reschedule or cancel a booked time, change its length or mark it a youth's, a warning when one runs into the next, and a form to add an appointment by hand.
- **Home**: the secretary's week, and the leader's day. See [Home](/docs/home/).

The settings behind it are in [Settings](/docs/settings/): [appointment hours](/docs/settings/appointment-hours/), [usual lengths](/docs/settings/usual-lengths/), [message templates](/docs/settings/message-templates/) and [who books for whom](/docs/settings/who-books-for-whom/).

## Who can see this

Interviews are limited to the bishopric by the database. Interviews and appointments never appear on any agenda. Messages and notifications are kept general and never say what an appointment is for. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
