This is the secretary's main job. Everything is on Home, under **Needs an appointment**, or on the Appointments tab.

## What you can do

- See open times that fit the leader's hours and the right length.
- Book a time, or offer a few times and wait for a reply.
- Message the person from a template, by text or email.
- Handle youth interviews by messaging a parent.
- Add an appointment by hand.

<Screenshot name="appointments-booking" device="phone" alt="Adding an appointment on the Appointments page." />

## Offer a few times

1. On **Home**, find the person under **Needs an appointment**. The line shows the kind of interview and how long they have been waiting.
2. Tap **Find a time**. Open times appear by day (tap **More days** for more).
3. Check the length. Use the **−** and **+** buttons to change it, in 5-minute steps. A temple recommend interview is at least 10 minutes.
4. Tap two or three times to choose them. A dot marks times already offered.
5. Tap **Message these times** (or **Message this time** for one).
6. Pick a **Template**. The message is filled in with their first name, the leader and the times. Change the text if you like.
7. Tap **Copy**, **Open Messages** or **Email**.

**Open Messages** opens your phone's messaging app with the text filled in and no recipient, so no phone numbers are stored in Ward Desk. Pick the person there.

Offered times are held for three days: nobody else's picker offers them until the person is booked, you tap **Release**, or the three days pass.

## Book a time

When the person replies:

1. Open **Find a time** again. The offered times are marked.
2. Tap the time they chose.
3. Tap the book button. The appointment moves to **To confirm**.

If you only pick one time you can book it straight away.

## Confirm it

Under **To confirm**, each booked time has a **Confirm** button that opens a confirmation message from a template. Sending it (Text or Email) confirms the time. If you already told them another way, tap **They already know**.

Once confirmed, the appointment leaves your Home and belongs to the leader: it shows on their Home, and afterwards they report how it went. Moving a booked time clears its confirmation and reminder, so the new time is confirmed and reminded again.

## Youth

For someone under 18, the secretary messages a parent instead.

1. Tick **Youth under 18: message a parent** (it is already ticked for youth interviews).
2. Enter **Parent's name**.
3. Messages now use the templates written to a parent, with the parent greeted by name and the youth's first name in the text.

Only the parent's name is kept, never a birthdate or phone number.

## Add an appointment by hand

On the **Appointments** tab, scroll to **Add an appointment**. Type the **Name**, choose who it is **With**, the **Purpose**, and a date and time. Leave the time blank to schedule it later; it then appears under **To schedule**.

## If a leader hasn't set hours

The card says the leader hasn't set appointment hours yet. Add them under **Appointment hours** (the bishopric can edit anyone's), or book a time directly. See [Appointment hours](/docs/settings/appointment-hours/).

## Who can see this

People with the bishopric role. A secretary sees the appointments of the leaders they schedule for, and everyone if they haven't been assigned. See [Who books for whom](/docs/settings/who-books-for-whom/).
