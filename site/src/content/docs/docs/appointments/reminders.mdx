The day before, the secretary sends each person a short reminder. Ward Desk collects them all on one page, each with its message ready.

## What you can do

- See every reminder due tomorrow, for appointments and for meetings.
- Copy, text or email each message.
- Mark one as sent, or undo.
- Remind everyone who attends a regular meeting.

<Screenshot name="reminders" device="phone" alt="The Reminders page with a message for each appointment ready to copy, text or email." />

## Send reminders

1. From 10 AM the day before, you get a notification, **Reminders for tomorrow**. Tap it, or tap the reminders card on Home.
2. On the **Reminders** page, find the person under **Appointments** or the meeting under **Meetings**.
3. Tap **Write reminder**. The message appears, filled in from a template.
4. Tap **Copy**, **Open Messages** or **Email**, and send it to the person.
5. The line then shows **Reminder sent**. Tap **Undo** if you tapped it by mistake. If you reminded them another way, there is a button for that too.

When everything is sent, the card on Home says "All sent. Nice work."

## Meeting reminders

You can have a regular meeting reminded the day before. In the [standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/), choose **Who reminds people the day before** for that meeting. That person gets the notification and the **Meetings** section here.

If the meeting is linked to its agenda (**Its agenda**), the reminder can name who is giving the opening prayer, the spiritual thought and the closing prayer that day, from the assignments on the agenda. Anyone not assigned yet shows as "(not assigned yet)", and the page links to the agenda to fill it in. See [Prayers and the spiritual thought](/docs/meetings/prayers-and-assignments/).

## Who can see this

Secretaries see the reminders for the leaders they schedule for. Reminder messages never say what an interview is for. See [Message templates](/docs/settings/message-templates/).
