After an interview, the leader marks how it went. This closes the loop, so the secretary knows whether anyone needs a new time.

## What you can do

- Mark an interview **Done**.
- Mark a missed one **Didn't come**, and either send it back for a new time or take care of it yourself.

## Report on your interviews

1. About half an hour after your last appointment of the day, you get a notification: **How did your appointments go?** It opens Home. (It is only sent if some are still unreported.)
2. Under **How did these go?** at the top of Home, tap **Done** or **Didn't come** for each.
3. For **Didn't come**, choose one:
   - **Find a new time**: the appointment goes back to the secretary under **Needs an appointment**, marked with the date it was missed and your note.
   - **I'll take care of it**: add a note and it is closed.

## Why it matters

A missed interview doesn't disappear. If you send it back, the secretary is notified, and the person is offered a new time.

## Who can see this

The bishop or counselor who held the interview and the secretaries who schedule for them. Notifications only say how many, never who.
