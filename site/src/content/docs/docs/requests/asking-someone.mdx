## For the person who is asking (bishopric)

### What you can do

- Send a request to a bishopric member or a ward leader.
- See if it has been seen, finished, or can't be done.
- Take a request back.
- Spot anything that has sat unread for two days.

### Send a request

1. Open **Requests** and tap **Ask someone to do something**. (From Home, the same button is under quick actions.)
2. **Who is it for?** Choose a person, grouped under **Bishopric** and **Ward leaders**. Tap **Next**.
3. **What would you like them to do?** Type a few words, for example "Set up chairs for the fireside".
4. Add **Details** and a **By when** date if you like.
5. Tap **Send request**.

The person gets a notification that doesn't say what it is about. The details stay in the app.

You can also hand an item on an agenda to someone. It becomes a request, and the agenda shows who has it. See [Working an agenda](/docs/meetings/working-an-agenda/).

### Follow along

Under **You asked**, each request has a tag: **Not seen yet**, **Seen**, **Done** or **Can't do it**, with their note if they left one. A request nobody has opened after two days is flagged. You can take back a request you sent with **Take back**, and the person won't see it anymore.

## For the person who is asked

### What you can do

- Tell them you've seen it.
- Tell them when it's done, or that you can't.

### Answer a request

<Screenshot name="requests-received" device="phone" alt="A request received: tap Got it, or It's done when finished." />

1. You get a notification, or see the count on the Requests tab. Open **Requests**. New requests are tagged **New**, and also appear under **Waiting for you** on Home.
2. Tap **Got it**. The sender can see that you've seen it.
3. When finished, tap **It's done**, add a note if you like (**Anything to add?**), and tap **Mark done**.
4. If you can't, tap **I can't do this**, say why (**Let them know why**), and tap **Send**.
5. If you tapped by mistake on a finished request, tap **Reopen**.

If a request has a due date, you'll get a reminder the day before, unless it's already done.

## Meeting assignments

Prayers and spiritual thoughts you are asked to give also appear on **Requests**, under **Meeting assignments**. You answer them with **Accept** or **Decline**. See [Prayers and the spiritual thought](/docs/meetings/prayers-and-assignments/).

<Screenshot name="requests-assignment" device="phone" alt="Asked to give a prayer: Accept, or decline and say why." />

## Who can see this

A request is visible to the sender, the person asked and, when both are in the bishopric, to the whole bishopric. Details of a request are only in the app, never in notifications. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
