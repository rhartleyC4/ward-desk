If you are the bishop or a counselor, Home is your day on one screen.

## What you can do

- See today's interviews and, on Sunday, whether you are conducting.
- Mark yesterday's interviews done, or missed, with a note.
- Ask for an interview, add something to a meeting, ask someone to do something, or tell the bishopric you will be away, all from the quick actions.
- See the week ahead and what rotations are due this month.

## What's on the screen

- **Warnings.** A red notice if a coming Sunday has no one from the bishopric at church (because of away dates), and a yellow one if only one of you will be there. See [Dates I'll be away](/docs/settings/dates-away/).
- **How did these go?** Appears after your appointments. Mark each **Done** or **Didn't come**. For a missed one, either send it back to the secretary to find a new time, or choose **I'll take care of it** and add a note, which closes it.
- **Today.** Your appointments with times, a "You're conducting" tag, and any rotation that falls today ("Your turn"). It also shows what is next.
- **What would you like to do?** The quick actions, below.
- **Coming up.** The next several days, same layout.
- **This month.** Rotations due this month, with the day they fall on or "Any time this month".
- **Waiting for a time** and **Waiting for you.** Interviews you asked for that a secretary hasn't scheduled yet, and requests that are waiting on you, with **Got it** buttons.

## Quick actions

### Ask for an interview

1. Tap **Ask for an interview**.
2. Type the person's name (for example, Sister Ames).
3. If they are under 18, tick **They're under 18 (messages go to a parent)** and type the parent's name. Only the parent's name is kept, never a birthdate or phone number.
4. Choose the kind of interview: temple recommend, extending a calling, youth interview, PPI, advancement, tithing declaration or something else.
5. Choose who it **Meets with**. It's you by default.
6. Send. A secretary finds a time and sends the message.

### Add something to a meeting

Pick a meeting, then the agenda section, type the item (and a date, if it is a calendar item). It lands on that agenda for the next meeting. See [Working an agenda](/docs/meetings/working-an-agenda/).

### Ask someone to do something

Opens **Requests** with a new request ready. See [Asking someone to do something](/docs/requests/asking-someone/).

### I'll be away

Opens your away dates. See [Dates I'll be away](/docs/settings/dates-away/).

## Who can see this

You see your own appointments and the bishopric's items. Other leaders' interviews are visible to the bishopric, and nobody outside the bishopric can see them. Notifications about your appointments show only a time and a count, never a name.
