Home is where the day starts. It looks different depending on your calling, because it only shows what you need.

## What you can do

- See today and the week ahead at a glance.
- Do the common things in one tap: ask for an interview, add something to a meeting, ask someone to do something, or say you will be away.
- Spot problems early, like a Sunday when no one in the bishopric will be at church.

## Which Home you get

| Your calling | Your Home | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Bishop, first or second counselor | Your own day: interviews, conducting, quick actions | [Home for the bishopric](/docs/home/bishopric-home/) |
| Executive secretary or assistant | "Your week": who needs an appointment, open times, reminders | [The secretary's Home](/docs/home/secretary-home/) |
| Ward clerk, other bishopric role | No Home. You start on Meetings. | [Meetings & agendas](/docs/meetings/) |
| Called leader | No Home. You start on Requests (or Meetings if you attend one). | [Requests](/docs/requests/) |

## The finish-setting-up card

Until you have done the one-time steps, a **Finish setting up** card sits at the top of Home. See [Finish setting up](/docs/getting-started/finish-setting-up/).

## The numbers on tabs

Tabs show a small count of things waiting for you, such as new requests or appointments to schedule. Open the tab and act on them, and the count goes down. On Home, the count tells you which card it is counting.

## Who can see this

Home shows your own appointments and the bishopric's schedule, so only people with the bishopric role and a bishop, counselor or executive secretary position have one. Details are on [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
