Executive secretaries and assistant executive secretaries land on **Home**, titled "Your week". It is built for scheduling the bishop's and counselors' interviews.

## What you can do

- See at a glance who needs an appointment and the open times that fit.
- Offer a few times, or book one, with a message ready to send.
- Confirm booked times and change their length.
- Send tomorrow's reminders.
- See each leader's hours for the next seven days.

<Screenshot name="home-secretary" device="phone" alt="The executive secretary's Home on a phone, with who needs an appointment and open times." />

## What's on the screen

- **Reminders for tomorrow.** A card that opens the [Reminders](/docs/appointments/reminders/) page when there is something to send.
- **Needs an appointment.** Everyone the bishopric has asked to see, for the leaders you schedule for, with open times by day at the right length.
- **To confirm.** Booked times that haven't been confirmed yet. Adjust the length with the − and + buttons; a warning shows when a time runs into the next one.
- **Bishopric this week.** Each leader's hours over the next seven days, with a link to edit them.
- **Who covers what** (folded). Each leader's areas, from the [standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/).

If you haven't been assigned to any leader, you see everyone. See [Who books for whom](/docs/settings/who-books-for-whom/).

## How to book a time

See [Book an appointment](/docs/appointments/book-an-appointment/) for the full steps.

## Who can see this

Only executive and assistant executive secretaries get this Home and the Appointments tab. Reminders and messages never say what an interview is for.
