A new bishop often needs the person before them for a few weeks. Mentoring lets the outgoing bishop keep access for a limited time, working as the new bishop.

## What you can do

- (Running the change) Let the outgoing bishop help, with a last day up to 12 weeks away.
- (Mentor) Switch between using Ward Desk as yourself and as the new bishop.
- (New bishop) Move the last day, allow callings, or end mentoring at any time.

## Turning it on

In the **Change of bishopric** steps, the **Help for the new bishop** question appears when the bishop is changing, the outgoing bishop is being released, and the new bishop has signed in to Ward Desk first. Choose **Let (name) help for a while**, set the **Last day** (4 weeks to begin with, 12 at most), and decide whether to **Include callings**. See [Changing the bishopric](/docs/leadership-changes/).

## How it works

- The mentor works **as** the new bishop. Everything they do is recorded as the new bishop's, so other people see the bishop's name, and the others in the bishopric are notified as usual.
- Only the bishopric role carries over, never admin.
- The new bishop's own name, sign-in devices and notification choices can't be changed by a mentor.
- It stops by itself on the last day, or sooner if the new bishop ends it, or if the new bishop leaves the bishopric.

## For the mentor

<Screenshot name="mentoring-mentor" device="phone" alt="The account page for a mentor, with a choice of using Ward Desk as yourself or for the new bishop." />

1. Open **Settings** and your name, and find **Use Ward Desk as**.
2. Choose **For (the bishop)**, with the date it lasts until, or **Yourself**.
3. While helping, a banner on every page says so. Tap **Be yourself** to switch back (or **More** if you have no calling of your own).

## Callings are private by default

Calling discussions stay hidden from a mentor unless the new bishop allows it. Instead of the callings, the agenda says "Callings are private while you help as a mentor." The new bishop can allow it later, or switch it off again.

## For the new bishop

<Screenshot name="mentoring-bishop" device="phone" alt="The account page for the new bishop, showing the mentor, last day and the Can see callings switch." />

On **Settings** then your name, **Your mentors** shows each person who can use Ward Desk as you, with:

- **Can see callings**: a switch.
- **Last day**: change it (within 12 weeks of the start) and save.
- An end button to stop mentoring right now.

You are told when your mentor changes something: for example "Your mentor changed an appointment for you", and a general "Your mentor helped" message. Notification text stays generic.

## Who can see this

Everything a mentor sees is what the new bishop sees, minus callings unless allowed. It is enforced in the database. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
