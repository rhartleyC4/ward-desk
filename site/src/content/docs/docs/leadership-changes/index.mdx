When the bishopric is released, or an organization gets a new president, the open work should move to the new person without anyone retyping it. Ward Desk does this in guided steps, one question per screen.

## What you can do

- Change the whole bishopric in one go: new bishop, counselors, secretaries and clerk.
- Change a single ward leader for an organization.
- See exactly what moves to whom before anything happens.
- Optionally let the outgoing bishop help for a while. See [Mentoring](/docs/leadership-changes/mentoring/).

## Who can do it

| Change | Who |
|---|---|
| **Change of bishopric** | The bishop, or an admin |
| **Change a ward leader** | Anyone in the bishopric, or an admin |

Both are under **Settings**, in the **Ward** group. A mentor helping the new bishop can't run a change of bishopric.

## Change of bishopric

Open **Settings**, then **Change of bishopric**.

<Screenshot name="handover-bishop" device="phone" alt="The first question of the change of bishopric: who is the new bishop?" />

1. **Who's the new bishop?** Pick someone who already signs in, or **Someone new, not in Ward Desk yet** and type their email. (There is no "leave it empty" for the bishop.)
2. **Who is first counselor?** and 3. **Who is second counselor?** For each you can **Keep** the current person, pick someone else, add someone new by email, or **Leave it empty for now**.
4. **Secretaries and clerk.** The same choices for executive secretary, assistant executive secretary and ward clerk.
5. **The people leaving.** For each person who no longer holds an office, choose **Released** (leaves the bishopric; an admin stays an admin) or **Stays as a ward leader** and pick the organization they now lead.
6. **Help for the new bishop.** Only asked if the bishop is changing, the outgoing bishop is being released and the new bishop has already signed in. See [Mentoring](/docs/leadership-changes/mentoring/).
7. **Check and confirm.** Every office, and for each person leaving, a list of the open work that will move.

Picking someone for one office takes them out of another, and the app stops you if someone is down for two offices.

Tap **Make the change**, then confirm. It happens straight away and can't be undone from the app.

If you added someone new by email, they are added to the list of people who can sign in, and their work waits until they first sign in. The last screen lists them so you can tell them.

## Change a ward leader

Open **Settings**, then **Change a ward leader**.

<Screenshot name="handover-leader" device="phone" alt="Change a ward leader: which organization?" />

1. **Which organization?** It shows who leads it now.
2. **Who leads it now?** Pick the new leader, or add someone new by email.
3. **And the leader before?** Choose what happens to them (skipped if nobody led it yet).
4. **Check and confirm**, then make the change.

## What moves to the new person

Everything still open that belonged to the person leaving an office goes to whoever takes it:

- open requests to and from them,
- appointments still to come or to be booked, including a calling's "Meets with" (booked ones need confirming again),
- calling requests not yet received, and a leader's own calling requests still in progress,
- rotations and each member's assigned areas (stewardships),
- who books for whom,
- usual appointment hours (only if the new person has none),
- Sundays they were down to conduct, and standing meetings they remind people about,
- agenda attendance.

An empty counselor office hands its work to the bishop, and an empty assistant secretary office to the executive secretary. Moving things sends no notification for each appointment.

## What stays

Finished requests and past appointments stay with whoever handled them. The name only shows **Former member** after that person's account is deleted. See [Who can sign in](/docs/settings/who-can-sign-in/).

## Who can see this

The check step shows names and counts to whoever runs the change. Nothing is sent to the Church, and the change is recorded as the person who ran it. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
