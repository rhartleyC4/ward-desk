The opening prayer, the spiritual thought and the closing prayer are given to someone picked from a list. That person is notified and taps **Accept** or **Decline**, so you know who is coming prepared.

## What you can do

- Pick who gives each part from a list of people who sign in to Ward Desk.
- See at a glance whether each has accepted, declined or not answered.
- For leaders who don't use the app, send a text or email and record their answer for them.

## Give out an assignment

1. Open the agenda on **Meetings** and the meeting date.
2. At the top, tap **Edit assignments**.
3. Choose a person for **Opening prayer**, **Spiritual thought** and **Closing prayer**, or leave **No one yet**.
4. Tap **Save**.

Each person you chose gets a notification asking them to accept or decline. The notification names the kind of thing, not the person's details. If you had typed in a name before the list existed, it is shown as "Typed in before".

Who is on the list: if you are in the bishopric, everyone who has a role in the ward. If you attend a meeting without being in the bishopric, you pick from the same people. Someone with no role can't be picked.

## If you are asked

You get a notification, and the assignment appears on **Requests** (under **Meeting assignments**) and on your Home.

1. Open it.
2. Tap **Accept**. Or tap **Decline**, add a short reason if you like (for example, "Out of town that week"), and send.
3. Changed your mind later? Tap **I can't anymore** and give a reason.

Whoever gave out the assignment is told your answer, and the agenda shows it:

- **Waiting for an answer** (yellow)
- **Accepted** (green)
- **Declined** (red, with your reason if you gave one)

## When someone doesn't use the app

Under the assignments, tap **Not using the app? Confirm for them**.

1. Find the person. A message is already written for you.
2. Tap **Text** or **Email**. Your phone opens with the message filled in, and nothing is stored.
3. When they answer, tap **They said yes** or **They said no** (add what they said). The agenda marks it "recorded by" you.

## Who can see this

An assignment can be read by the people who attend that meeting and by the person asked. Notification text never includes names. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
