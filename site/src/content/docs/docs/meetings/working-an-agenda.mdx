An agenda is meant to be used during the meeting and kept up between meetings.

## What you can do

- Add a discussion item or a dated calendar item.
- Mark an item **Done**, which takes it off the next agenda.
- Hand an item to someone so they can report back.
- Add or remove names on the prayer roll.

## Add an item

1. Open **Meetings** and pick the agenda.
2. Find the section, for example "Action / discussion items".
3. Type your note in the box at the bottom of the section and add it. In a calendar section there is a date field as well.

The item stays on the agenda, week after week, until it is marked done.

## Mark an item done

Tap **Done** beside the item. It is cleared from the section and doesn't come back. A calendar item drops off by itself after its date.

You can also add an item from Home: tap **Add something to a meeting**, choose the meeting and the section. See [Home for the bishopric](/docs/home/bishopric-home/).

## Hand an item to someone

Some items are really tasks. To give one to a person:

1. Tap the person icon beside the item (its label reads "Ask someone to do …").
2. Choose **Who?** and, if you like, a **By when** date.
3. Tap send.

The person sees it on their **Requests** page and answers there. The item on the agenda then shows who has it and where it stands. See [Asking someone to do something](/docs/requests/asking-someone/).

## Follow-ups

The bishopric agenda has a **Follow-ups** section. It lists requests between members of the bishopric that are still open, and ones finished in the last week, so you can ask about each in the meeting.

## Look at other weeks

The arrows at the top show the previous and following meeting. **Upcoming** returns to the next one. You can also open a past agenda to see what was covered.

## Who can see this

Anyone who attends the meeting can read and add to its agenda. Callings are shown only to the bishopric, even on a shared agenda.
