Each meeting that has an agenda, such as the bishopric meeting or ward council, gets one page on the **Meetings** tab. The agenda is put together live from what is already in Ward Desk, so there is no weekly copy-and-paste.

Everyone can follow the agenda on their own phone during the meeting. When someone adds an item or marks one done, it shows on everyone else's screen within a second, with no need to reload.

## What you can do

- Open the agenda for the next meeting, or move to the previous or following week.
- Add items, mark them done, and hand an item to someone as a request.
- Give out the prayers and the spiritual thought, and see who has accepted.
- On the bishopric agenda, work on callings: see [Callings](/docs/callings/).

## How an agenda is made

An agenda is a list of sections. Each kind of section fills itself in:

| Section shows | What fills it in |
|---|---|
| Open items | Anything you add, carried forward week after week until someone marks it **Done** |
| Calendar | Dated items, which drop off once the date has passed |
| Assignments | Rotations that are due this month (from the [standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/)) |
| Sacrament meetings | Upcoming Sundays from the [Sacrament](/docs/sacrament/) tab |
| Callings, Releases, Sustainings | The callings work, only for the bishopric. See [Callings](/docs/callings/) |
| Prayer roll | A shared list of names to remember, the same on every agenda that shows it |
| Closing prayer | The closing prayer for that day |
| Follow-ups | Requests among the bishopric that are still open, and ones finished in the past week |

Your ward can have more than one agenda. If you attend several meetings you'll see a tab for each at the top. Use the arrows to move one meeting back or forward, or **Upcoming** to jump to the next one.

## Who can see this

**Agendas follow attendance, not roles.** The bishopric can open every agenda. Anyone else sees only the agendas they have been added to. Someone who attends ward council can read and edit that agenda, but still can't see callings or appointments. The callings section shows nothing to anyone outside the bishopric. Interviews and appointments never appear on an agenda. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
