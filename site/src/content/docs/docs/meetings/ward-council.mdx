Ward council has its own agenda, with a section for each organization. Leaders added as attendees can read and edit it, even though they are not in the bishopric.

## What you can do

- Add and clear items for your organization.
- See the prayer roll and who has the prayers.
- As a leader, ask for a calling change without seeing the bishopric's private work. See [Calling requests](/docs/callings/calling-requests/).

<Screenshot name="agenda-ward-council-leader" device="phone" alt="A ward leader's view of the ward council agenda." />

## If you attend ward council

You'll see a **Meetings** tab and **Requests**, and nothing else. Open **Meetings** and the ward council agenda is there. The prayer roll is shared with any other agenda that shows it.

You can't see:

- the callings section,
- appointments or interviews,
- other agendas you haven't been added to.

## Add a leader to an agenda

An admin or bishopric member opens [Agendas](/docs/settings/agendas/) in Settings, finds the meeting and picks the person under **Attendee**.

## Who can see this

Only the bishopric and the people added as attendees. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
