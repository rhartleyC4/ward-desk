## What you can do

- Choose who presides and who conducts.
- Enter the hymns, the invocation and benediction.
- Build the program: talks, musical numbers, an intermediate hymn, testimonies.
- Track each speaker from an idea to a confirmed talk.
- Reorder the program with the up and down arrows.

<Screenshot name="sacrament" device="phone" alt="Upcoming Sundays on the Sacrament tab of a phone." />

## Plan a Sunday

1. Open **Sacrament** and find the Sunday.
2. Tap **Plan** (or **Edit** if it already has a plan).
3. Choose the **Type**. See [Fast Sunday and conferences](/docs/sacrament/special-sundays/).
4. Fill in **Presiding**, and pick who is **Conducting** from the bishop and counselors.
5. Type the hymn numbers for **Opening**, **Sacrament** and **Closing**.
6. Fill in the **Invocation** and **Benediction**.
7. Under the program, add each item and pick its kind: **Talk**, **Musical number**, **Intermediate hymn**, **Testimonies** or **Other**.
8. For a talk, enter the **Speaker** and **Topic**, and set a **Status**: **Idea**, **Invited**, **Confirmed** or **Declined**. Anything not confirmed shows a small status tag so you can see who still needs asking.
9. Use the up and down arrows to reorder, or the bin to remove an item.
10. Tap **Save**.

## Warnings

If the person conducting has put an away date on that Sunday, the card says so: "… is away this Sunday". Fix it by choosing someone else, or ask them to change their dates. See [Dates I'll be away](/docs/settings/dates-away/).

## Prayers here and on the agenda

The invocation and benediction on this page are just text. The prayers and spiritual thought on the **bishopric meeting agenda** are separate, and people are asked and accept there. See [Prayers and the spiritual thought](/docs/meetings/prayers-and-assignments/).

## Who can see this

The plan is visible to everyone with a role except called leaders, and editable by the bishopric. Names of speakers appear on this tab but never in notifications, which only say that there is a sacrament meeting to conduct. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
