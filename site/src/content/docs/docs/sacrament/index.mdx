The **Sacrament** tab shows a card for each of the next twelve Sundays, so speakers and hymns are never planned at the last minute.

## What you can do

- See who is presiding and conducting, the hymns, and the program for each Sunday.
- Plan or edit a Sunday (bishopric only).
- Mark Fast Sundays and conference weekends.
- Be warned when whoever is conducting is away.

## Pages in this section

- [Plan a Sunday](/docs/sacrament/plan-a-sunday/): presiding, conducting, hymns, speakers.
- [Fast Sunday and conferences](/docs/sacrament/special-sundays/): the Sunday types and what changes.

## Who can see this

Everyone with a role except called leaders can read the plans for upcoming sacrament meetings. Only the bishopric can plan or edit them. Prayers and spiritual thoughts for the bishopric meeting are separate and live on the agenda. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
