Ward Desk is a small app for the people who run a ward: the bishopric, executive secretaries, the clerk and other called leaders. It keeps agendas, callings, sacrament meeting plans, appointments and requests in one place, on your phone or a computer.

These pages are short and task-based. Start with the one that sounds like you.

## I want to try it first

- [Try the demo](/docs/getting-started/try-the-demo/): practice accounts with invented people, nothing to set up.
- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started/): signing in, installing the app and the first few things to set up.

## I use it in my calling

| If you are... | Start here |
|---|---|
| The bishop or a counselor | [Home for the bishopric](/docs/home/bishopric-home/), then [Meetings & agendas](/docs/meetings/) |
| An executive secretary or assistant | [The secretary's Home](/docs/home/secretary-home/), then [Appointments](/docs/appointments/) |
| The ward clerk | [Setting apart and recording](/docs/callings/sustaining-and-setting-apart/) |
| A ward leader (Relief Society president, Elders Quorum president and so on) | [Requests](/docs/requests/) and [Calling requests](/docs/callings/calling-requests/) |
| The ward admin | [Who can sign in](/docs/settings/who-can-sign-in/) and [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/) |

## I want to know who can see what

[Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/) has a table of every role and feature, and explains how confidentiality is enforced in the database, not just hidden on screen.

## I want to run it for my ward

[Running it for your ward](/docs/self-hosting/) explains what you need, what it costs in effort, and that each ward that runs a copy is responsible for its own data.
