## What you can do

- Add the dates you will be away.
- See who else is away.
- Take your away days out of your appointment hours automatically.
- Be warned when a Sunday would be left without the bishopric.

<Screenshot name="settings-away" device="phone" alt="The away page, listing who is away and when." />

## Add away dates

1. Open **Settings**, then **Dates I'll be away** (or tap **I'll be away** on Home).
2. Under **I'll be away**, enter **From** and **Through**, and **Where (optional)**.
3. Tap to save.

Your away days are taken out of your appointment hours, so a secretary won't offer times on them. Sacrament plans show a warning if whoever is conducting is away.

To remove an entry, tap the bin beside it under **Upcoming**.

## Short-handed Sundays

When away dates leave a Sunday with no one from the bishop and counselors, or only one, Ward Desk warns the whole bishopric: a red notice if no one will be there, a yellow one if only one will, naming which one by office. The same warning shows on this page, on Home, and as a notification (**Short-handed Sundays**, which you can switch off).

If you are about to add dates that would leave a Sunday empty, the page tells you before you save.

## Who can see this

Away dates are readable by anyone with a role other than called leaders. They show where you will be only if you type it in **Where**.
