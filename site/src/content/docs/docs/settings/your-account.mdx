Open **Settings** and tap your name at the top.

## What you can do

- Change the name others see.
- Set up Face ID or fingerprint sign-in on this device.
- See, rename and remove the devices that can sign you in.
- Choose [notifications](/docs/settings/notifications/).

<Screenshot name="settings-account" device="phone" alt="The account page: your name, Face ID and fingerprint, and notifications." />

## Change your name

1. Edit **Your name**. It is how you appear on agendas, requests and appointments.
2. Tap **Save**. It shows "Saved."

You can't change the email you sign in with. If that has to change, an admin removes the old address and adds the new one. See [Who can sign in](/docs/settings/who-can-sign-in/). Your position (such as executive secretary) is set by an admin too.

## Set up Face ID or fingerprint

1. Under **Face ID and fingerprint**, tap **Set up this device**.
2. Approve the prompt from your phone.

It is added to the list with a name that says what kind of device and browser it is, such as "iPhone · Safari · iOS 26", and when it was added. The one you set up on the device you're holding is marked **This device**.

Browsers don't reveal an exact model on Apple devices, so two iPhones can look the same. Tap the pencil to give a device a name you'll recognize, like "Work phone". Tap the bin to remove one (for example, a lost phone). You can still sign in with an email link. See [Signing in](/docs/getting-started/signing-in/).

If your browser can't do it, the page says "This browser can't use Face ID or fingerprint sign-in."

## Who can see this

Your name is seen by the people who use Ward Desk in your ward. Your passkeys are visible only to you.
