Ward Desk is invite-only. This page is where an admin decides who can sign in and what they can do.

## What you can do

- Add someone's email address with a role.
- Change their roles and bishopric position.
- Remove someone's access.
- Delete an account entirely.

<Screenshot name="settings-access" device="phone" alt="The list of people who can sign in, with their roles and positions." />

## Add someone

1. Open **Settings**, then **Who can sign in**.
2. Under **Add someone**, type the **Email**.
3. Choose their roles, by tapping the chips:
   - **Bishopric**: plans meetings and sees callings (bishop, counselors, secretaries, ward clerk)
   - **Ward leader**: suggests names for callings. Pick their **Organization** too, which is shown here and used to route and fill in their calling requests.
   - **Admin**: manages meetings and who can sign in
4. If you chose Bishopric, pick a **Position**: Bishop, First Counselor, Second Counselor, Executive Secretary, Assistant Executive Secretary or Ward Clerk. The position decides which Home they get.
5. Tap **Add**.

They can now sign in with that email, with Google or an email link. Changes apply right away, even for people who have already signed in. You can't remove your own admin access.

## Changing a leader

To replace someone who leads an organization, or the whole bishopric, use the guided steps instead, so their open work moves too. See [Changing the bishopric or a ward leader](/docs/leadership-changes/).

## Remove someone

Tap the remove button beside their email under **Who can sign in** and confirm. They can't use the app until they're added again. Their account stays until you delete it, under **Removed, account kept**.

## Delete an account

Under **Removed, account kept**, tap **Delete account** and confirm.

This deletes the person's sign-in, profile, roles, away dates, appointment hours and notification settings, and takes their email off the list. It can't be undone. Requests and calling requests they were part of stay, shown as "Former member".

## Who can see this

Admins only. The database enforces it. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
