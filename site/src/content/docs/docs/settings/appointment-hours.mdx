The bishop and counselors keep appointment hours here. Secretaries offer times only inside them.

## What you can do

- Set your usual weekly hours, in blocks.
- Change a single day: different hours, or a day off.
- Take a day out for a leader who says they can't make it.

<Screenshot name="settings-hours" device="phone" alt="Appointment hours: usual weekly blocks and the next two weeks." />

## First time: two short steps

If you're a bishop or counselor with no hours yet, you land here at sign-in.

1. **Pick every day that works** (for example Sunday and Tuesday). Tap **Continue**.
2. Set the **From** and **To** times for each day. These are a starting point; you can change them later or add a second block on the same day.
3. Tap to save. Or tap **Add them one at a time instead**.

## Usual hours

Under **Usual hours**, each block is a day with a start and end. Add another by choosing a **Day** and times, then **Add**. Tap the bin to remove one, for example "Sunday 9 to 11 AM".

## Change a single day

Under **Next two weeks**, find the day and tap **Change**.

- Add hours for that day only. They replace the usual hours for it.
- Tap **Day off** to take the whole day out.
- Tap **Reset** to go back to the usual hours.

Away dates also take a whole day out. They come from [Dates I'll be away](/docs/settings/dates-away/).

## Edit someone else's hours

Anyone with the bishopric role can edit anyone's hours. Choose a person in **Hours for** at the top, so a secretary can take a day out when a leader says they can't make it.

## Who can see this

People with the bishopric role. Called leaders can't see appointment hours.
