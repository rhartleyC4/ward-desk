Each meeting with an agenda, such as the bishopric meeting or ward council, is set up here.

## What you can do

- Create a new meeting with a day, weeks of the month and time.
- Add, rename, reorder and remove sections.
- Choose who attends besides the bishopric.

<Screenshot name="settings-meetings" device="phone" alt="Meetings in Settings: each meeting with its sections and attendees." />

## Create a meeting

1. Open **Settings**, then **Agendas**.
2. Tap **New meeting**.
3. Enter the **Meeting name**, **Day**, **Time**, and tick the weeks of the month it meets (or all of them).
4. Tap **Create meeting**.

## Add a section

1. On the meeting, choose what the new section **Shows**:

| Shows | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Open items | Discussion items that carry forward until marked done |
| Calendar | Dated items that drop off after the date |
| Assignments | Rotations due this month |
| Sacrament meetings | Upcoming sacrament meetings |
| Callings (with Releases and Sustainings) | The callings work, for the bishopric |
| Prayer roll | The shared prayer roll |
| Closing prayer | The closing prayer for the day |

2. Give it a **Section title** and add it.
3. Use the arrows to reorder it, the pencil to rename it, or the bin to remove it.

A section's kind can't be changed after you add it. Add a new section and remove the old one instead.

## Choose who attends

The bishopric always sees every agenda. Under **Attendee**, add anyone else who attends, such as the Relief Society president for ward council. They can then open that agenda from **Meetings**, and nothing else.

## Safeguards

- A section can only be removed once all its items are marked done. Its finished items go with it.
- A meeting can only be deleted if it has never had an agenda or item. The bishopric meeting can never be deleted.

## Who can see this

The bishopric and admins. Called leaders can't create or change agendas. See [Meetings & agendas](/docs/meetings/).
