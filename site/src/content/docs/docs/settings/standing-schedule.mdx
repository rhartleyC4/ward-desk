The standing schedule is what repeats. It feeds Home, the agendas and the day-ahead reminders.

## What you can do

- List regular meetings and when they happen.
- Keep rotations, such as which leader interviews the Elders Quorum president.
- Record each bishopric member's areas, so "Meets with" fills in for callings.

<Screenshot name="settings-schedule" device="phone" alt="The standing schedule: regular meetings, rotations and bishopric assignments." />

## Regular meetings

1. Open **Settings**, then **Standing schedule**, and tap **Add a meeting**.
2. Enter the **Meeting** name, **Which weeks** (every week, or one week of the month), the **Day** and an optional **Time**.
3. Optionally, pick **Who reminds people the day before**. They get a notification the morning before, with the reminder ready to send. See [Reminders and confirmations](/docs/appointments/reminders/).
4. Optionally, link **Its agenda**, so the reminder can name who has the prayers and spiritual thought that day.
5. Save.

## Rotations

A rotation is something that takes turns or is due in certain months.

1. Tap **Add a rotation**.
2. Enter the **Rotation** name, for example "PPI: Elders Quorum President".
3. Choose **Whose turn**: a bishopric member, or **Someone else** (type a label like "EQ President"; this reminds the whole bishopric).
4. Tick the months it is due, and choose the **Week** (a set day such as the 3rd Sunday) or any time that month.

Home shows a rotation on its day, and lists the month's under **This month**.

## Bishopric assignments

Under **Bishopric assignments**, add the areas each bishopric member oversees, picked from the calling list's organizations or typed in. When a name is approved for a calling in one of those organizations, **Meets with** fills in for that leader. If two leaders share an area, it won't fill in. See [Approvals and "Meets with"](/docs/callings/approving-names/).

## Keep titles general

Meeting and rotation titles are shown **exactly as typed on lock screens** in the day-ahead summary. Ward Desk checks as you type and warns "This looks like it has a name in it" if a title contains a name. Use "Primary message", not a person's name.

## Who can see this

Everyone with a role other than called leaders can see the schedule. The bishopric and admins can change it. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
