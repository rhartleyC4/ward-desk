Ward Desk can tell you when something needs you, without opening the app. Notifications are on the **Account** page, below your sign-in settings.

## What you can do

- Turn notifications on for each phone or computer you use.
- Check that this device can show one.
- Choose which kinds you receive.

<Screenshot name="settings-notifications" device="phone" alt="Notification choices grouped by Your day, Sundays, Appointments, Requests and Callings." />

## Turn them on

1. Open **Settings**, tap your name, and scroll to **Notifications** and **This device**.
2. Tap **Turn on for this device** and allow notifications when your phone asks.
3. Tap **Show a test** to check. If nothing shows, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device's settings and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is off.
4. **Turn off** switches this device off again.

On an iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later), notifications only work from the Home Screen app. See [Install on your phone](/docs/getting-started/install-on-your-phone/).

## Choose what you get

Below, each kind of notification has a switch. Everything starts on, and your choice applies on every device. You only see the switches that could apply to you.

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Your day** | **Tomorrow's summary** in the evening; **How did today go?** after your last appointment; **Reminders to send** |
| **Sundays** | **Short-handed Sundays**, when away dates leave the bishopric short |
| **Appointments** | New appointments to book, missed and sent back, booked or moved times |
| **Requests** | New requests, due tomorrow, answers, meeting assignments |
| **Callings** | New calling requests, callings to record (clerk), request progress (leaders) |

## The day-ahead summary

Each evening between 6 and 10 PM your time, bishopric members get one notification about the next day: standing meetings and their times, your rotations, conducting sacrament meeting, how many appointments you have, a note if you're away, and how many requests are waiting. Nothing is sent for a day with nothing on it.

## Notifications are kept private

Notifications show on lock screens, so they say when and how many, and name an office, never a member or what an appointment is for. If you set up a meeting or rotation title, it is shown exactly as typed, so keep it general. See [Standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/).

## Who can see this

Only you see your switches and devices. A notification can only go to the person it is for.
