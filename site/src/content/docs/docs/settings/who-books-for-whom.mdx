## What you can do

- Say which executive secretary or assistant schedules each leader's appointments.
- Pick more than one secretary for a leader.

<Screenshot name="settings-who-books" device="phone" alt="Choosing which secretary books for each leader." />

## How to set it

1. Open **Settings**, then **Who books for whom**.
2. For each leader (the bishop and counselors), tick the secretary or secretaries who schedule for them.

Each secretary's Home then starts with the leaders they cover.

## When you don't have to

If there is only one executive secretary, they book for everyone and the page just says so. A leader nobody has been picked for falls to the executive secretary. It only matters once you have an assistant to share the leaders with.

## Who can see this

People with the bishopric role who can manage meetings.
