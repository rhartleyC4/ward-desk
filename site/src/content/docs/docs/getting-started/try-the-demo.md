The quickest way to see Ward Desk is the public demo at **[demo.warddesk.app](https://demo.warddesk.app)**. It is a full copy of the app filled with invented people and made-up appointments, so you can click anything without worry.

## What you can do

- Sign in as a bishop, a counselor, a secretary, a clerk or a ward leader, and see how each one's screens differ.
- Try every feature: plan an agenda, approve a name, book an appointment, send a request.
- Switch between accounts to see both ends of a request or a calling request.

## Sign in to the demo

1. Open [demo.warddesk.app](https://demo.warddesk.app). A banner at the top says it is a training site.
2. On the sign-in screen, tap anyone in the list. There is no password to type.

To see someone else's view, pick them from the list in the banner at the top. You don't need to sign out first.

## A good tour

1. Sign in as **Bishop Avery**. Open **Meetings** and look at the callings on the bishopric agenda. Then open **Requests** and ask someone to do something.
2. Switch to **Brother Quinn** (secretary). Accept the request on **Requests**, then open **Home** to see who needs an appointment.
3. Switch to **Brother Hollis** (Elders Quorum president). Notice that you see only Requests and the ward council agenda, with no callings or appointments. Ask for a calling change.
4. Switch back to the bishop and find your calling request on the bishopric agenda.

## Good to know

:::caution
**Never type real names or real member information into the demo.** Anyone can sign in with the practice accounts, so whatever you enter can be seen by anyone else using it. The demo is reset from time to time, which wipes everything entered since the last reset.
:::

- The practice accounts have no real email or phone, so messages the app prepares (texts and emails) have nowhere real to go.
- Your own ward's copy is a separate site. Nothing from the demo carries over. See [Running it for your ward](/docs/self-hosting/).
