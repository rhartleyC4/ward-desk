Ward Desk is invite-only. A ward admin adds your email address and gives you a role. After that, getting going takes about five minutes.

## What you can do

- Sign in with an email link, Google, or Face ID or fingerprint.
- Add Ward Desk to your phone's Home Screen so it opens like any other app.
- Follow the short **Finish setting up** checklist.
- Find your way around the tabs and Settings.

## The first five minutes

1. **Ask to be added.** Your ward admin (often the bishop or the executive secretary) adds your email on the [Who can sign in](/docs/settings/who-can-sign-in/) page. You can't sign in with an address that isn't on the list.
2. **Sign in.** See [Signing in](/docs/getting-started/signing-in/).
3. **Add it to your Home Screen.** See [Install on your phone](/docs/getting-started/install-on-your-phone/). On an iPhone this is required for notifications.
4. **Work through the checklist.** See [Finish setting up](/docs/getting-started/finish-setting-up/).

## Finding your way around

The tabs at the bottom of a phone (along the top on a wider screen) hold everyday work. Which tabs you see depends on your role:

| Tab | What it is | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Today, quick actions and the week ahead | The bishop, counselors and executive secretaries |
| Meetings | The agendas for meetings you attend | Anyone who attends a meeting with an agenda |
| Sacrament | Plans for upcoming sacrament meetings | Everyone except called leaders |
| Appointments | Interviews to schedule and booked times | Executive secretaries |
| Requests | What you have been asked to do | Everyone with a role |

Things you set up once, such as your name, appointment hours and notifications, live in **Settings**, which opens from your initials at the top right.

## If you see "no role has been assigned"

You are signed in, but your email was added without a role. Ask your ward admin to add one. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/) for what each role does.

## Who can see this

Nothing here shows other people's information. The tabs you see are the parts of Ward Desk your role allows.
