Only email addresses on your ward's list can sign in. There are no passwords to remember.

## What you can do

- Get a sign-in link by email.
- Sign in with Google, if your ward has turned that on.
- Sign in with Face ID or a fingerprint, once you have set it up on a device.

## Sign in with an email link

1. Open your ward's Ward Desk address.
2. Type your email and tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email on the same device and tap the link. You are signed in.

If the message says your address can't sign in, it is not on the list yet. Ask your ward admin to add it.

## Sign in with Google

If you see **Sign in with Google**, tap it and choose the Google account that uses the same email address your ward added. The list of allowed addresses still applies: a Google account that isn't on the list is refused.

## Turn on Face ID or fingerprint

After you sign in once, Ward Desk offers to turn on Face ID or fingerprint sign-in for that device.

1. Tap **Turn on** when the banner appears. (Tap **Not now** if you would rather wait. You can set it up later in Settings.)
2. Approve the prompt from your phone.
3. Next time, tap **Face ID or fingerprint** on the sign-in screen and skip the email.

Why this matters on an iPhone: an email link always opens in Safari, not in the app on your Home Screen. A Face ID sign-in happens inside the Home Screen app, so you stay in it. See [Install on your phone](/docs/getting-started/install-on-your-phone/).

Passkeys sync through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager, so a new phone usually has them already. They only work on the address they were made for.

### See, rename or remove your devices

Open **Settings**, tap your name at the top, and look under **Face ID and fingerprint**. Each device you set up is listed with the date it was added and last used. Tap the pencil to give one a name you'll recognize. Tap the bin to remove one, for example if a phone is lost. You can always sign in with an email link instead. See [Your account](/docs/settings/your-account/).

## Sign out

**Settings**, then **Sign out** at the bottom.

## Who can see this

Only you. Signing in doesn't share anything with other people in the ward.
