Until you have finished the one-time steps that apply to you, Ward Desk shows a **Finish setting up** card on Home, a red dot on your initials at the top right, and the same checklist at the top of **Settings**.

## What you can do

Tick off up to three steps, in this order. Each shows only if it applies to you and your device.

| Step | Who sees it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Set your appointment hours** | The bishop and counselors | So the secretaries know when to book you |
| **Turn on Face ID or fingerprint** | Anyone whose device supports it | Sign in without waiting for an email |
| **Turn on notifications** (or **Add Ward Desk to your Home Screen** on an iPhone) | Everyone with a role | Requests, appointments and your day, as they happen |

## How to finish them

1. Tap the **Finish setting up** card on Home (it shows the next step and a progress ring), or open **Settings**.
2. Tap the step marked **Next**. It takes you to the right page.
3. Complete it. The step is crossed off, and the card disappears when all are done.

The pages behind each step:

- [Appointment hours](/docs/settings/appointment-hours/)
- [Your account](/docs/settings/your-account/) (Face ID)
- [Notifications](/docs/settings/notifications/)

A bishop or counselor who has not set hours is taken straight to the hours page after signing in, with two short questions: which days, then what times.

## Who can see this

Only you. Your checklist depends on your role and your device.
