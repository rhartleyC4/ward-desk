Ward Desk is a web app, so there is nothing to download from an app store. You add it to your Home Screen and it opens full screen like any other app.

## What you can do

- Open Ward Desk from its own icon, signed in.
- Get notifications on your phone. On an iPhone this only works once the app is on the Home Screen.
- See the number of things waiting for you on the app icon, where the phone supports it.

## On an iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later)

Use Safari.

1. Open your ward's Ward Desk address and sign in.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow). If you don't see it, tap the ••• first.
3. Tap **View More**, scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.
5. From now on, open Ward Desk from its icon. Then [turn on notifications](/docs/settings/notifications/) from inside it.

Ward Desk shows these steps itself in an **Add this app to your home screen** banner. Tap the × (**Not now**) to hide it.

:::tip
After installing, sign in once inside the app with an email link, then turn on Face ID. An email link opens in Safari, so Face ID is the easy way to sign in to the Home Screen app. See [Signing in](/docs/getting-started/signing-in/).
:::

## On Android

1. Open the browser menu (⋮). On Samsung Internet, it is ≡ at the bottom.
2. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.
3. Open it from its icon.

If your browser offers it, the banner in Ward Desk has an **Install** button that does this in one tap.

## On a computer

Nothing to install. Open the address in any current browser. On a wide screen the tabs move to the top and Home, Requests and agendas use the extra room.

## Updates

Ward Desk checks for a new version when you bring it back to the front, so you normally don't have to do anything. If something looks out of date, close and reopen it.

## Who can see this

Only you. Installing doesn't give the ward any information about your phone beyond the notification address your phone provides when you turn notifications on.
