When the bishopric decides on a name, you approve it. Approval is what starts the conversation with the person.

## What you can do

- Approve one name for a calling.
- Choose which member of the bishopric meets with them.
- See whether the appointment is waiting to be scheduled, booked, or met.
- Step back if you approved the wrong name.

## Approve a name

1. Find the name in its calling's card.
2. Tap **Approve** beside the name. Until a name is approved, this is the only button: a name can't be accepted or declined first.
3. The name is now **Approved**, **Meets with** appears, and the card shows **Interview**.

Only one name per calling can be approved at a time. The other names show "Another name is approved" until you step back.

## Who meets with them

**Meets with** is filled in for you when it can be. Ward Desk picks the bishop or counselor whose assignment on the [standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/) names that calling's organization, so a counselor over the Primary meets with the Primary calling. It leaves it blank when:

- someone already meets with them,
- the calling isn't on the calling list, or
- two leaders share the organization.

You can change it at any time from the **Meets with** picker, which lists the bishop and counselors. Choose **—** to take the appointment back.

The line under the picker says where it stands: **Waiting to be scheduled**, a date and time, or **Met**.

## What the secretary sees

An approved name with a leader appears on the secretary's Home under **Needs an appointment**, with no time yet. The secretary who books for that leader finds a time. See [Book an appointment](/docs/appointments/book-an-appointment/).

## Change your mind

Each name shows one button for its next step. The rarer choices are in the **⋯** menu beside the name:

- For an approved name: **Declined**, or **Back to considering** if you approved the wrong name.
- For an accepted name: **Undo accepted**, which takes it back to approved and clears the dates it set.

**Declined** is final.

## Who can see this

Only the bishopric. The secretary sees the person's name and the appointment, because they schedule it, but not the calling discussion. Notifications never include names.
