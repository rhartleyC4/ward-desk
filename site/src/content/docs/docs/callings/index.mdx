Callings are worked on in the **Callings** section of the bishopric agenda, in the bishopric meeting. There is no separate page to remember: the list is in front of you when you need it, and what you decide flows to appointments, sustainings and releases.

## What you can do

- List the callings that need to be filled and who is being released.
- Add names to consider, including names suggested by ward leaders.
- Approve a name and say which bishopric member will meet with them.
- Record that they accepted, and keep track of the sustaining and the release.
- Mark the person set apart, which finishes the calling.

<Screenshot name="agenda-bishopric-callings" device="phone" alt="The callings section of the bishopric agenda, showing callings to fill and the names being considered." />

## The path a name takes

1. **Considering.** A name is added, by you or by a leader's suggestion.
2. **Approved.** The bishopric approves it. A "Meets with" leader is chosen and the secretary is asked to schedule an appointment.
3. **Accepted.** The person accepts. The sustaining and release dates are set and the appointment is marked met.
4. **Sustained.** On the sustaining Sunday the name shows under **Sustainings** (and the release under **Releases**). It then waits to be set apart.
5. **Set apart.** You tap **Set apart**, and the calling is finished and leaves the list.

A name can also be **Declined**, which is final.

## Who can see this

**Calling proposals are confidential.** Only people with the bishopric role can see names, statuses and decisions. A ward leader can send a request and follow how far along it is (sent, received, being worked on, finished) but never sees names or decisions. This is enforced in the database. See [Roles & privacy](/docs/roles-and-privacy/).
