## What Ward Desk keeps about a person

- Your **email** and **name**, and your roles and position.
- Your **away dates** and, if you are a bishop or counselor, your **appointment hours**.
- Your notification choices and the devices you turned notifications on for, and your Face ID or fingerprint sign-in devices (public keys only, never your face or fingerprint).
- Records that involve you: requests you sent or received, assignments, agenda items you added.

The people being discussed (for callings, interviews and so on) are entered by the bishopric as names only. No birthdates, phone numbers or addresses are kept.

There is no advertising or tracking in the app.

## You can

- Change your own name in [Your account](/docs/settings/your-account/).
- Remove a Face ID device, or turn off notifications, at any time.
- Ask an admin to remove you. Removing an email stops access. An admin can then delete the account, which deletes your sign-in, profile, roles, away dates, appointment hours and notification settings. Requests and calling requests you were part of stay, shown as "Former member".

You can't change your sign-in email yourself: an admin removes the old address and adds the new one.

## The demo

The public [demo](/docs/getting-started/try-the-demo/) holds invented data only. Never put real information in it.
