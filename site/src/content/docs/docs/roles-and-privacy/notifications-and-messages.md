A notification can pop up on a phone left on a table. Ward Desk is written with that in mind.

## Notifications

- They say **what kind of thing** happened and **when** or **how many**: for example how many appointments you have tomorrow and from when to when, or "The bishop asked you to do something".
- They name an **office**, never a member, and never what was asked or what an appointment is for.
- The day-ahead summary lists meetings and rotations by the title **you typed**. Ward Desk warns you if a title looks like it contains a name. Keep titles general. See [Standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/).
- Nothing is sent for a day with nothing on it.

Each person chooses which notifications they get. See [Notifications](/docs/settings/notifications/).

## Texts and emails

Reminders and confirmations are drafted from [templates](/docs/settings/message-templates/) the bishopric controls, and sent from the sender's own phone or email app. Ward Desk does not send them, and **does not keep phone numbers**. For a youth under 18, the message goes to a parent, and only the parent's name is kept.

Templates should stay general, so a message never says what the appointment is for.

## Sign-in emails

The emails that carry sign-in links use generic wording with no names.

## Alert emails

If something breaks, the system emails the person who runs the ward's copy with counts and where to look. The alert never includes names or error text.
