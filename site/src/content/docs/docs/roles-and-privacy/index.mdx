Ward Desk gives each person a **role**, and the role decides what they can see. Roles are kept in Ward Desk's own database, not in Google or anywhere else, and an admin sets them on the [Who can sign in](/docs/settings/who-can-sign-in/) page.

## The three roles

| Role | Who it's for | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Bishopric** | The bishop, first and second counselors, executive secretaries and assistants, and the ward clerk | Plans meetings, sees callings, schedules appointments, sends requests |
| **Ward leader** | Other called leaders, such as the Relief Society or Elders Quorum president | Sends calling requests with suggested names and follows them; answers requests sent to them |
| **Admin** | Whoever manages the ward's copy (often the bishop or executive secretary) | Decides who can sign in; can also hold other roles |

A person can hold more than one role. Within the bishopric role, a **position** (Bishop, First Counselor, Second Counselor, Executive Secretary, Assistant Executive Secretary, Ward Clerk) decides which Home they get and which pickers they appear in. Only an admin can set a position.

**Meeting attendance** is separate from roles. A ward leader who is added as an attendee of ward council can read and edit that agenda.

## Role by feature

✓ means yes. "Own" means only their own. "Attendee" means only meetings they have been added to.

Some notes on the table:

- The bishopric sees **every** agenda; everyone else sees only the meetings they have been added to, plus the shared prayer roll if one of those agendas shows it.
- A leader who attends ward council still can't see callings or appointments.
- A calling request is sent to the bishop or counselor whose assignment on the [standing schedule](/docs/settings/standing-schedule/) names that organization, or the bishop when no one's does.
